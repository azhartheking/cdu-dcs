How to use these checklists

  1. Use a new printed copy for every start-up or shutdown. Fill in the header fields before you begin.
  2. Do the steps in order. After each step check the Verify column on the DCS, write the actual reading, tick ☑ and write the time and your initials.
  3. If a verification fails, stop. Return the unit to a safe state, record it in the deviation record (page 7) and inform the shift supervisor.
  4. At a HOLD POINT the shift supervisor checks the unit condition and signs before anyone continues.
  5. Any step you skip or change must be recorded in the deviation record, with the reason and approval.
